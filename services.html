Services

Ynforma is a creative design studio. Every project starts with a conversation about what you need, then a written quote.

Concept art

Visual development for games, apps and other creative projects: characters, environments, props and mood and style exploration.

UX design

Interface and interaction design for apps and tools: layouts, flows, visual style and usability review.

Soundtrack composition

Original music for games, videos and other media: themes, background music and soundtrack pieces.

Game design

Designing fun game mechanics: core gameplay loops, systems, progression, balance and prototypes.

Story and narrative writing for games

Game stories, characters, worldbuilding, quest and dialogue writing.

Consulting

Advice and review for your game or digital project: design critique, feature and mechanics feedback, UX review, and creative direction for small teams.

How to commission

Send a short description of the project through the contact page. I reply with questions and a written quote before any work or payment. See the policies for payment, revisions and refunds.
