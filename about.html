Founder, Ynforma · online as ynformal

Ynforma is run by Dmitry Alexander Samoilov, a designer and developer.

Ynforma is a creative design studio. It brings together game design, narrative writing, concept art, UX design and music composition, so one studio can shape a project from its first idea to its finished feel.

Ynforma also builds free, open source visual browsing tools. The code is at github.com/dmitryalexander, where I go by ynformal.
