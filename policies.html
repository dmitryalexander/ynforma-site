Policies

Commissions (game design, writing, art, UX, music, consulting)

  • Scope, price, deadline and number of revisions are agreed in writing before work starts.
  • Payment terms (for example a deposit and a final payment) are stated in each quote.
  • Refunds and cancellations: a deposit is non-refundable once work has begun. If you cancel, you are refunded any prepaid amount for work not yet started, and you pay for work already completed. If I cannot deliver what was agreed, I will refund the amount paid for the undelivered work.
  • Rights and credit for the finished work are stated in each quote.

Sponsorship

  • Sponsoring my open source work is voluntary and processed by GitHub Sponsors.
  • You can change or cancel a sponsorship at any time from your GitHub sponsorship settings.
  • Sponsorship supports ongoing development. It does not buy ownership of the software, which stays under its open source licenses.

Questions or disputes

Contact me through the contact page and I will respond and work it out with you.
